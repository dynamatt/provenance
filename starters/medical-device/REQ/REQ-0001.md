---
id: REQ-0001
type: Requirement
title: "Example: non-contact measurement accuracy"
rationale: The accuracy clinical thermometers are expected to meet.
verification_methods:
  - test
status: draft
implements:
  - USR-0001
---
The thermometer shall measure body temperature within ±0.2 °C between
35.0 °C and 42.0 °C, from 3 cm to 5 cm from the forehead.
