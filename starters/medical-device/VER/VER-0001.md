id: VER-0001
type: VerificationProtocol
title: "Example: accuracy against a blackbody"
steps:
  - description: Set the blackbody source to 35.0 °C and take ten readings at 4 cm.
    acceptance_criteria: Every reading is within ±0.2 °C of 35.0 °C.
  - description: Repeat at 37.0 °C, 39.0 °C and 42.0 °C.
    acceptance_criteria: Every reading is within ±0.2 °C of the set temperature.
status: draft
verifies:
  - REQ-0001
---
Place the thermometer on a fixture 4 cm from the aperture of a calibrated
blackbody source, at an ambient temperature of 23 °C ± 2 °C.
